# R3 audit: STK (store, GRN, inventory, PO, material request, supplier invoice, stock lock)

Read-only audit, 2026-10-13. Items fixed in C1-C7 or listed deferred in 00-README.md are not repeated. Symbols are cited, not line numbers.
Money visibility was checked on every reader in scope (getGRN, listGRNs, getPO, reconcilePO, listSupplierInvoices, listStoreItems, listStockSummary, getStoreDashboard, commitReceipt rate, updateStoreItemProps, createItemAction, import route, GRN alerts, audit writer). No server-side money leak to non-OWNER was found. Only STK-23 (client-derived money) touches the money rules.

| ID | Sev | file:symbol | Defect | Failure scenario | Proposed fix | Schema? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| STK-01 | Major | server/mis/grn.ts:confirmGRN | Confirm increments `receivedQuantity` but never moves the PO status. Only `store.ts:commitReceipt` sets PARTIAL/COMPLETE. | A PO fully received through the GRN screen stays APPROVED forever. It stays in the "New GRN" picker and `listOpenPOs`. Supplier "completed POs" reads 0, and the PO list never shows Complete. | In the confirm tx, after the increments, recompute PARTIAL/COMPLETE exactly as `commitReceipt` does (extract one shared helper). | N |
| STK-02 | Major | grn.ts:createGRN, confirmGRN | No PO status guard on the server. The "approved only" filter exists only in `grn/page.tsx`. Any `poId` (DRAFT, PENDING_APPROVAL, CANCELLED, COMPLETE) can get a GRN, be confirmed, and book stock. | A crafted action call, or a stale tab on a PO cancelled meanwhile, books stock against an unapproved or cancelled PO. `cancelPO` only counts GRNs, so it races with a new GRN. | Require APPROVED/RECEIVING/PARTIAL in `createGRN` and re-check inside the confirm tx after the PO-line locks. | N |
| STK-03 | Major | store.ts:commitReceipt | The receive cart has no over-receipt cap and no PO status check. It takes the PO line lock for neither `poItemLockKey` nor outstanding quantity. It books to the first unclaimed PO line with the same `itemId`, even if that line is already full. | Store Guy receives 500 against a line with 100 outstanding. `receivedQuantity` becomes 500 of 100, the GRN cap in `confirmGRN` is bypassed, and a concurrent `confirmGRN` races on the counter. A receipt can also go against a COMPLETE or CANCELLED PO. | Lock `poItemLockKey`, call `assertWithinOutstanding`, pick the line with room, and require an open PO status. Share the helper with grn.ts. | N |
| STK-04 | Major | store-books.ts:nextTxnSeq (callers: createStoreIn/Out, reconcile, commitReceipt/Issue, confirmGRN) | The daily `STXN-yyyymmdd-nnnn` number is read as max+1 under per-ITEM locks only. `txn_number` is UNIQUE. Two simultaneous movements on different items read the same max. | Store Guy issues item A while a GRN confirms item B. Both get `STXN-...-0007`. One tx rolls back with a raw P2002 and the user retries blindly. | Take one global advisory key (e.g. `stxn-seq`, sorted in with the item keys) before `nextTxnSeq`, or retry on P2002. | N |
| STK-05 | Major | grn.ts:nextGrnNumber (used by createGRN, commitReceipt) | The number is `GRN-yyyymm-` + the last 5 digits of epoch ms. It has no attempt offset and no retry, unlike PO/MRN/order numbers. `grn_number` is UNIQUE. The suffix repeats every 100 s, and it uses local `getFullYear`. | Two receipts a multiple of 100 s apart in the same month collide (birthday odds grow with GRN volume). `createGRN` throws a raw unique error, or `commitReceipt` rolls back a whole delivery. | Use the `nextPoNumber(attempt)` pattern plus a P2002 retry loop in both callers. | N |
| STK-06 | Major | inventory.ts:adjustInventory | A manual adjustment writes only `mis_inventory_ledger`. The storekeeper's book gets nothing, and `notes` (the reason) is optional. | After "+50" on Inventory, `/mis/store/stock`, count, and the picker disagree. `createStoreOut` then refuses at min(ledger, txn). A "-5" removes stock the store book still shows, so issue and receive balances drift permanently. | Write the matching store-transaction row in the same tx (as `reconcilePhysicalCount` does), and require a reason of at least 3 chars. | N |
| STK-07 | Major | inventory/adjust-dialog.tsx + inventory/actions.ts:adjustInventoryAction | No try/catch, no `asResult`, and no error state in the dialog. The server throws "Adjustment would result in negative balance". | In production the message is replaced by the generic Next error. The slide-over stays open, nothing is saved, and the user gets no explanation (or the error boundary). | Wrap the action in `asResult`, `unwrap` in the dialog, and show the inline error. | N |
| STK-08 | Major | store.ts:commitIssue | The balance check uses the ledger only. The store book is written `txnBalance - qty` and can go negative, with only `isOverIssue` set. Spec D2 says refuse everywhere, and `createStoreOut` already uses min(ledger, txn). | With the books out of step (legacy items, STK-06), a cart or material-request approval drives the storekeeper's balance below zero. `/store/stock` shows negative stock. | In the cart pre-check, use `min(ledgerBalance, lastTxnBalance)` and drop `isOverIssue`. | N |
| STK-09 | Major | store.ts:reconcilePhysicalCount (with recordPhysicalCount) | Reconcile applies the stored `discrepancy` from count time, not the variance against the current balance. It checks "once" per count, not per item. | Count says 90 vs system 100 (delta -10). Eight units are issued, leaving 92. Reconcile still applies -10, giving 82, but the physical stock is 90. Two counts of one item on one day can each be reconciled, which double-corrects. | In the tx, take `physicalQty - current balance` under the lock, or refuse when the book moved since `count.createdAt`. Block a second reconcile per item per count day. | N |
| STK-10 | Major | grn.ts:confirmGRN vs addGRNItem / updateGRNItem / updateGRNHeader | Confirm reads the GRN and its lines BEFORE the transaction and books from that snapshot. `addGRNItem`, `updateGRNItem`, and `updateGRNHeader` check DRAFT first, then write with no status guard in the write itself (`removeGRNItem` has one). | A second user adds a line while the first confirms. The line lands on a CONFIRMED GRN and is never booked to stock, so the receipt is silently short. An edited quantity booked from the stale snapshot is the same bug. | Re-read lines inside the tx after the DRAFT to CONFIRMED claim. Make the create/update writes conditional on `grn.status = DRAFT` (updateMany or relation filter). | N |
| STK-11 | Major | supplier-invoice.ts:matchFor / reconcileSupplierInvoice, grn.ts:confirmGRN (upsert) | The invoice row links to a single GRN. A later GRN naming the same invoice overwrites `grnId`, and the match runs on that one GRN's lines against the invoice total. | An invoice covering two deliveries shows DISCREPANCY (qty/price) as soon as the second GRN confirms, or after any Reconcile click. The false alarm flows to the Owner's GRN alert and the board. | Match against all confirmed GRNs of the PO that name this `invoiceNo` (query by `po_id` + `supplier_invoice_no`). | N |
| STK-12 | Minor | grn.ts:confirmGRN (invoice upsert) | Uniqueness is `(poId, invoiceNo)`. The same supplier invoice number entered on a second PO creates a second record with no warning. | A duplicated or re-keyed supplier invoice number goes undetected, so the same bill can be paid twice against two POs. | Warn at header save when `(supplierId, invoiceNo)` already exists on another PO; a hard constraint later. | Y (hard), N (warn) |
| STK-13 | Minor | store/actions.ts (storeIn, physicalCount, updateStoreItem, deactivateStoreItem), inventory/actions.ts (createItem), suppliers/actions.ts + customers/actions.ts (delete/restore), supplier-invoices/actions.ts | Server actions throw instead of returning `asResult`. In production Next replaces the message, so the UI's `e.message` catch shows a generic text. | "Quantity must be positive", "Item not found or inactive", "Supplier not found", and duplicate item codes all reach the user as "An error occurred...". The delete/restore buttons have no catch at all, so they fail silently. | Wrap each in `asResult` and `unwrap` in the screens (the pattern already used in grn/po/requests actions). | N |
| STK-14 | Minor | store.ts:createStoreIn / createStoreOut / recordPhysicalCount, inventory.ts:adjustInventory | Quantities are not rounded or validated like the cart (`mergeCartLines`). `NaN <= 0` is false, `0.004` passes then stores 0.00, and `physicalQty` is unchecked server-side (the UI blocks negatives only). The discrepancy is raw float subtraction. | A bad or edge value is stored as a zero or NaN txn. The ledger balance (`round2`) and the stored `quantity` (Decimal rounding) can disagree by 0.01 on `x.xx5`. A negative physical count saved via a direct call can never be reconciled. | Use `positiveQty` (and finite and ≥0 for counts); round the discrepancy to 2 dp. | N |
| STK-15 | Major | api/mis/inventory/import/route.ts | Matching an existing item by code or sku overwrites `category` and `unit` with defaults OTHER/PIECE whenever those columns are blank or unrecognised. It also matches deleted or inactive items and "updates" them silently. Skipped rows return the raw Prisma message. | Re-importing a price sheet with only Name and Code turns RAW_MATERIAL items into OTHER/PIECE. OTHER is then refused for order issue by `categoryRefusal`, and the unit changes under existing stock history. | Update only the columns that are present and valid, skip deleted items, and map errors to a friendly reason. | N |
| STK-16 | Minor | inventory/actions.ts:createItemAction, import route (code generation) | The auto code is `ITM-` + (`misItem.count()` + 1) (`count + i` in import). The count includes soft-deleted rows and ignores gaps or imported codes. No name or duplicate validation. | A duplicate code means a P2002 raw error from the action (see STK-13). Two concurrent creates take the same number. | Generate from max existing code with a P2002 retry, and validate the name. | N |
| STK-17 | Minor | store.ts:updateStoreItemProps | `reorderLevel` (and an Owner's price) accepts negative or NaN (`parseFloat('abc')` in the UI). `category` is an unvalidated free string cast into an enum. | Reorder level -5 flags every item low or never low. An invalid category throws a raw Prisma enum error. | Validate finite, non-negative values and an enum membership check. | N |
| STK-18 | Major | po.ts:addPOItem / updatePOItem | No validation of quantity, rate, description, or `itemId`. Negative, zero, or NaN quantity and rate are accepted, as is an empty description, an unknown or soft-deleted item, or a catalog-less line. `hasBillableLine` only needs one line above zero. | A PO with qty 100 plus a -50 line, or a negative rate, computes a wrong total and approval mode (`sumPoItems` float sum vs threshold). A zero-qty line can never be received. | Validate qty > 0 (rounded 2 dp), rate ≥ 0 (2 dp), a non-empty description, and an active item on add and update. | N |
| STK-19 | Minor | po.ts:submitForApproval / approvePO | A PO can be submitted and approved with no supplier and with lines lacking a catalog item. The no-catalog-item block only appears at GRN confirm (D4), after the goods are already on the dock. | Goods arrive against a supplier-less PO, then confirm is refused by D4. The storekeeper cannot fix a frozen approved PO. | Require supplier and itemId on every line at submit. | N |
| STK-20 | Minor | po.ts:submitForApproval / approvePO / cancelPO / updatePO | Status transitions are read, check, then `update({ where: { id } })` with no status in the write. | Double-click or two admins: BOTH mode step 1 stamps twice (admin step overwritten), and cancel races a new GRN (`misGrn.count` then update). | Use `updateMany` with `status: expected` and treat count 0 as "already changed". | N |
| STK-21 | Minor | supplier.ts:deleteSupplier / restoreSupplier, customer.ts:deleteCustomer / restoreCustomer | No guard for open dependants and no already-deleted check. | STK-23 | Minor | store-stock-screen.tsx (`totalValue`), store.ts:listStockSummary / getStoreDashboard | Money is derived client-side (`price * balance` reduce), and the server sends raw unrounded floats (`stockValue`, `totalStockValue`) instead of formatted strings.
