# R3 audit: ORD (orders, BOM, production, QC, phases, machines, clearance, reports, CoA, print)

Read-only audit. All items verified in code; no line numbers (grep the symbol). None of these are in C1-C7 or the deferred list.

| ORD-01 | Major | server/mis/orders.ts:updateOrderStatus (CANCELLED / COMPLETED) | Closing or cancelling an order touches nothing that depends on it: open `MisMachineAllocation` rows, `MisOrderStockAllocation` reservations, open material requests, PENDING_APPROVAL BOM, PENDING phases. | ORD-07 | Major | qc.ts:recordAqlSample + lib/mis/aql.ts:evaluateAql | `sampleSize` 0, NaN (UI does `Number(x) \|\| 0`) or below the required size still yields ACCEPT and a PASS `MisQcCheck`; `sampleSizeMet:false` lives only in the audit snapshot. | ORD-08 | Major | qc.ts:addQcCheck, qc-template.ts:recordChecklistCheck | No validation of `result` value, `defectQty` (negative/NaN), `bomStageId` belongs to the order's BOM (production does check this), order exists/open, FAIL without a defect type. | ORD-09 | Major | print/coa/[id]/page.tsx (overallResult) | "Overall Result" counts every historic FAIL even when later re-passed (the close gate and `MisQcCheck` doc define resolved = later PASS on same stage+parameter). | ORD-12 | Major | reports/page.tsx:monthRange; reports.ts range filters | Month range built with server-local `new Date(y, m-1, 1)` to `23:59:59`; factory is Asia/Kolkata (D22) and the host is UTC.
