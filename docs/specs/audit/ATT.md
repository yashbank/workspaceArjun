Read-only audit. Files are under `src/server/mis/` unless stated. Android paths are under `mis-kiosk-android/app/src/main/java/com/example/mis_kiosk/`. `PunchViewModel.kt` was not touched.

## Not re-reported / checked and clean
- Punch idempotency itself holds: the key is claimed first inside the transaction (`runIdempotent`), duplicates return the original result, a lost race is handled, and the DB refuses punch edits.
- Wages isolation holds for the surfaces read: payroll, payslip, the month wage bill, extra-pay listing and close are `wages.read`. The payroll page strips money before the client, and audit payloads omit figures (D24).
- Kiosk auth is sound: bearer SHA-256 + `timingSafeEqual`, the token is returned once, and revoked devices are refused on pull and photo (but allowed on punch by design).
- The pull allow-list holds: `PULL_EMPLOYEE_KEYS` is built key by key with no money.
- Android: `allowBackup=false` plus the extraction rules, encrypted credential prefs, and `BASIC` logging (no headers) are all correct.
